# Architecture

`game.c` is a freestanding RV32IMAC program that uses only PRG32's portable cartridge ABI (`skipass_madness_init/update/draw`). It is built with the 128 KiB cartridge RAM profile (about 34 KiB code and 51 KiB RAM) and uses no heap, no floating point and no initialized pointers. Portable cartridges are not relocated, so sprite descriptors are filled in at run time and the code avoids even local pointer arrays.

## Course model

Each piste is a table of 10 m segments `{length, slope, bend, half-width}` (`SEGS`). `build_course` rescales the slopes so that the total drop matches the real start and finish altitudes (`TOP_M`, `BOT_M`), smooths them, and fills per-step tables at a 10 m `STEP`:

| Table | Holds |
|---|---|
| `c_alt` | altitude |
| `c_sin`, `c_cos` | slope sine and cosine |
| `c_phi` | bend angle |
| `c_u`, `c_v` | world centreline, integrated from the bend angle |
| `c_hw` | half-width |
| `prof` | 64-point altitude profile for the HUD |

`place_objects` uses a seed per piste, so every networked console sees the same layout.

## Physics (`physics_step`, 33 ms fixed steps)

Units are cm and cm/s, Q12 trigonometry, and angles in 1/16 degree. Sub-step accumulators keep slow motion from truncating to zero.

- Gravity along the heading: `g·sinθ·cos(h)`.
- Coulomb friction, `μ·g·cosθ`. Groomed snow is μ≈0.045, ice 0.02, moguls 0.105 and powder 0.17. The snowplough adds 0.3, the hockey stop 0.63, and skidding (turning faster than the skis carve) adds more.
- Drag `v²/k`, with separate values for upright, tuck and plough.
- The turn rate is capped by a 13 m carving radius, with lower caps in a tuck, on ice and in the air.
- Kickers and crests launch a ballistic flight. A hard or crossed landing is a fall.

The MAESTRO `autopilot()` scores lateral lines across the piste against every hazard in the next ~2 s, predicting where moving people will be, and takes the cheapest line. It drives the demo mode and the optional steering assist.

## Rendering

- **Background:** sky gradient, the Maiella-like ridge, hills, valley bands and villages, shifted by camera yaw and pitch.
- **Terrain:** 52 depth slices whose spacing grows geometrically. Each slice is projected from the camera behind and above the skier. A voxel-style `ymin` occlusion means crests hide the snow behind them.
- **Objects:** trees, poles, banners, huts, lifts, snowcats, cannons, patches and people are gathered into a far-to-near draw list, and each item is clipped to the occlusion line of its slice.
- **Colour:** everything is indexed. Backgrounds use palette slots 8–15 and 232–255 set with `prg32_palette_set`. Sprites and text use the console colour cube.

## Audio

`assets/audio.json` is generated by `scripts/generate_audio.py`. It holds 16 SID-like synthesised instruments with no PCM samples:

- Instruments 0–7 play the three stereo tracker tracks (title/demo, win, game over). The tracker plays only in those modes.
- During play, `audio_quiet()` stops the tracker, and `ski_audio()` drives instruments 8–15 on held and one-shot voices: hiss, wind, carving scrape, thud, crash, whoosh and chime, with live volume and pan.

## Multiplayer

Each console publishes its local state every frame:

| Field | Contents |
|---|---|
| `x` | lateral position, cm |
| `y` | distance down the piste / 20 cm |
| `sprite` | piste, plus pose << 4 |
| `flags` | mode, plus km/h << 4, plus 0x8000 when finished |

Peers on the same piste are drawn on the slope and on the profile panel, and ranked.

## Testing notes

- `tests/host` compiles `game.c` natively for frame renders, autopilot timing and ASan/UBSan fuzzing.
- `scripts/multiplayer_test.py` runs two QEMU consoles against a local multiplayer server. It gives each console its own flash and NVS, and checks the room traffic and both framebuffers.
- The QEMU firmware stalls while its multiplayer client retries a server it can't reach. The scripts therefore start QEMU with `-nic user,model=open_eth,net=<compiled subnet>,guestfwd=tcp:<compiled host:port>-cmd:nc 127.0.0.1 8081` and an SDL display; headless `-display none` also hangs.
- The QEMU audio stream is mono, so the preview's audio is a mono mix of the stereo output.
